⚔️ Natac: War ← Settlement game

Inspired by The Quest for El Dorado: your army only moves as far as the cards in your hand allow. Every card is a type of supply. Spend Rations to march infantry, Fuel to drive armor, artillery and aircraft, Ammo to attack, and Funds to buy better cards from the shared market. Build your deck, take the supply depots, and storm the enemy HQ.

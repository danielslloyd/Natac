Battle log

How to play

Two armies start at opposite HQs (⭐). Win by moving infantry or armor onto the enemy HQ, or by destroying every enemy unit. After 40 rounds, whoever holds more depots (⛺) wins.

Each turn

  1. Play cards. Click a card in your hand to add its value to a supply pool. Wild cards ask which pool to fill.
  2. Move. Select a unit, then click a green tile. The number on the tile is its cost, paid from 🥫 (infantry) or ⛽ (everything else). Each unit also has a per-turn movement limit.
  3. Attack. With a unit selected, click a red-ringed enemy. Each attack costs 💥 ammo, and a unit can't move after attacking. Hover first to see the damage you'll deal and take.
  4. Buy. Buy one card per turn. 💰 Funds pay full value, and any other card from your hand counts as ½. New cards go to your discard pile and come round when you reshuffle.
  5. End turn. Pools empty, cards you played go to your discard pile, and you draw up to 4 cards (+1 for each depot you hold, max 6).

Tips

  • Units heal 2 HP per turn on your HQ or a depot you hold.
  • Aircraft must end their move within 4 tiles of your HQ or a depot you hold. When you select one, the darker area shows where it can't stay.
  • Trash weak starter cards with Reorganization or Staff Officer to make your deck stronger.
  • Hotseat: when two humans play, a hand-off screen hides each player's hand between turns.
